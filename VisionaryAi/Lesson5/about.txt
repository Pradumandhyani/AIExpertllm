Image filtering is the process of modifying an image by applying a
 mathematical operation (called a filter or kernel) to improve its 
 appearance, extract useful information, or create special visual effects.

In simple words:

Image filtering means changing the pixels of an image to achieve a desired result.

For example, you can:

Make an image brighter or darker.
Blur an image.
Sharpen details.
Detect edges.
Remove noise.
Change colors (red, blue, green tint).
Real-Life Example

Imagine you're looking at a photograph.

If you wear red sunglasses, everything appears reddish.
If you wear blue sunglasses, everything appears bluish.
If you clean a blurry camera lens, the picture becomes sharper.
